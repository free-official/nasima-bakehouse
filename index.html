PLEASE READ

Order information & policies.

Clear information helps us understand your request and avoid confusion.

Pricing

Listed prices are per pound for the stated flavours. Custom design charges vary by design and complexity. The bakery confirms the final quotation before accepting an order.

Order confirmation

A chat or enquiry is not a confirmed order. Your order is accepted only after the bakery confirms availability, final price and arrangements.

Custom designs

Share your preferred theme, wording, colours or reference photo. The bakery will review the request and advise whether it can be made and the applicable charge.

Timing & collection

Please share your required date and time as early as possible. Preparation time, delivery availability and collection arrangements must be confirmed with the bakery.

Changes & cancellation

Contact the bakery as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel a request. Any applicable terms will be confirmed when the order is discussed.

Privacy

Contact and order details shared with us are used to respond to your enquiry and coordinate your cake order. Please do not share passwords, OTPs or payment card details.
